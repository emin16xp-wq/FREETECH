🖱️ PC control

41 tools for smart computer use — with his own virtual mouse (ghost clicks that never move your cursor): mouse (incl. drag), keyboard, screenshots (full screen or one window), read/write/search files, recycle-bin deletes, window management (minimize/maximize/move/resize), launch & force-close apps, open URLs, processes, clipboard, notifications, volume, system stats — and a raw PowerShell tool for anything else. You SEE him work: a glowing Orbit cursor glides across your screen and ripples when he clicks. With a 👁 vision model he looks at the screen, aims at the exact button, clicks, and double-checks with a new screenshot. He can also read text off your screen (OCR), control media & volume, and indexes your whole game & app library (Start Menu, Steam, Epic, Store, registry), launches games through the right launcher, and every sensitive action shows an Allow / Deny prompt unless you choose full auto.

Works on Windows via built-in PowerShell — nothing to install. Try asking: "take a screenshot, then open Notepad and type hello".

📞 Call mode — talk to Orbit like a phone call

Press Call (the phone icon in the chat, or the button below) and just talk. Orbit hears you instantly, transcribes with Groq Whisper the moment you pause, answers, and speaks the reply out loud — hands-free, back and forth. Press End call any time to stop listening.

🎤 Voice input (speech to text)

Click the mic button in the chat, speak, click again (or just pause) — your words are transcribed with Groq Whisper and sent. Needs a Groq API key (free tier works) in the Connection tab.

API keys and settings are stored locally on this PC only (). Chats go directly from this app to the provider you chose. MCP servers run locally as child processes.

Quick tips

• Drag the orb to move it, click to chat, right-click for a menu.
• The hotkey works over games and fullscreen apps (borderless-windowed).
• In chat, click the model pill to switch model/provider any time.
• Exclusive fullscreen games will cover the orb — switch the game to borderless/windowed mode to see it.
